# Morning Brief

Establish the user's day and time zone. Gather only the requested calendars, tasks or status sources. Separate confirmed commitments, time-sensitive actions and missing feeds. Cite timestamps and avoid creating appointments.

## Illustrative request

Prepare a brief when the calendar is unavailable.

## Expected result

List verified work items and identify the calendar coverage gap.
