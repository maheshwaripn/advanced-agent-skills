# Studio Assemble Qc

Lock the shot order and audio revision. Check actual duration, missing frames, captions, audio sync, format and playback. List defects by timecode and severity. Mark delivery pending when assets or playback are unavailable.

## Illustrative request

A 30-second cut loses its last caption on export.

## Expected result

Identify the affected time range and require playback of the corrected export.
