# Quant Study Design

Translate the idea into a falsifiable hypothesis, point-in-time dataset and benchmark. Identify leakage, costs, multiple testing and sampling uncertainty. Compare development results under stressed assumptions. Label untested hypotheses clearly.

## Illustrative request

An indicator was selected after trying twenty settings.

## Expected result

Record all trials and require independent confirmation before crediting the apparent advantage.
