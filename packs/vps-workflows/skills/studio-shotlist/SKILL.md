# Studio Shotlist

For every screenplay beat choose framing, action, duration, audio cue and continuity reference. Total durations and track which beats lack coverage. Assign stable shot IDs for revisions.

## Illustrative request

Cover a radio repair and a reaction in twelve seconds.

## Expected result

Two or more numbered shots add to twelve seconds and preserve the hand holding the radio.
