# Studio Audio

Prepare cue in/out points, dialogue, room tone, music provenance and the delivery loudness target. Mix only when assets and tools exist. Check clipping, sync, transitions and intelligibility, and record measured versus intended levels.

## Illustrative request

A chime masks a spoken line near second eight.

## Expected result

Move or attenuate the cue, then verify the line and peak level on the new mix.
