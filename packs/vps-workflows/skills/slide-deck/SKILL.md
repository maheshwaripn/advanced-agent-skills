# Slide Deck

Define the audience and takeaway per slide, draft a sequence, then build editable slides where appropriate. Render the deck to check legibility, contrast, clipping and reading order. Verify data labels against source values.

## Illustrative request

Turn three status tables into an executive update.

## Expected result

A short deck shows the decision, trend and supporting evidence with readable labels.
